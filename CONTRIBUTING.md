# Participation in Gemina

Gemina welcomes independent study, criticism and reproducible research. The owner decides whom to collaborate with, which PRs to review and what to accept. Public access and open licenses do not grant write access or guarantee a response, review or merge. You may study, reuse and develop the licensed materials independently under their applicable terms.

1. Read [README](README.md), [project memory](docs/START-HERE.md), [AGENTS](AGENTS.md) and the sources relevant to your task. The current stage is **Research / Architecture Draft, documentation only**. A plan is not authorization to implement or run experiments in the maintained project.
2. Use a separate branch and a focused PR, initially Draft for ongoing work. External contributors can use a fork; invited collaborators and agents can use their own branches. Preserve existing PRs and unrelated edits. Do not push to `main`, rewrite history or merge without the owner's explicit authorization.
3. State the question, sources, base commit, changes, unresolved disagreements and actual checks. Separate hypotheses, owner decisions, analytical arguments and measured results. Keep `NOT_RUN` for checks not executed. No model has privileged authority over evidence.
4. Independent reviewers should record their own reasoning before reading another review when independence matters. Implementation and validation are separate roles; reviewing a plan does not validate code. Identify the agent/tool and relevant version when known, what it inspected and what a human verified. Do not invent a model name or co-author.
5. Preserve original sources, especially the DOCX. Put corrections in derived documents and record what they supersede. Include only material you have permission to submit; mark third-party material and its terms. Original contributions are submitted under the applicable [project license](LICENSE): MIT for software, CC BY 4.0 for research documentation. No separate CLA is required.

No functioning implementation or CI exists in the checked base. Future experimental PRs should specify a fixed protocol, code/dependency revisions, data provenance and splits, seeds, environment, budgets, commands, raw results and failures, plus controls that distinguish field participation from usefulness. Reproduction instructions belong with the actual artifacts; no runnable training command is claimed today.

Connecting Codex, Claude or another service is a separate owner action. Prefer read access for review, then only the permissions needed for an authorized task, scoped to **Gemina_project**. Never assume access to Project_Index or another repository. Do not include credentials or private runtime data in a PR. AI providers' data-use settings must be assessed when connecting them.
